FAQ

Questions, answered.

Useful details about supported links, access and responsible use.

How do I download an Instagram Reel?

Copy the public Reel URL from Instagram, paste it into the downloader above, select Get video and then use Download MP4 when the media is ready.

Can InstaFlow download private Instagram content?

No. InstaFlow is intended for public media you own or are authorized to save. It does not bypass private-account, subscriber or other access restrictions.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The interface is responsive and works in modern mobile browsers. The final save behavior can vary slightly by browser and operating system.

Do I need an Instagram account?

The website does not ask visitors to enter Instagram login credentials. Availability can still depend on Instagram's public-access limits for a specific post.

Is InstaFlow affiliated with Instagram?

No. InstaFlow is an independent utility and is not endorsed by, sponsored by or affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
